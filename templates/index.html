About the Research Prototype

Key functions provided by the maize disease incidence system.

07 / ABOUT
🧠

Machine-Learning Estimation

Uses trained regression models to estimate measured disease incidence from the available field inputs.

🗺️

Geographical Visualisation

Displays field record locations on an interactive map of the study areas in Kenya.

📊

County-Level Analysis

Summarises recorded incidence and provisional risk-category distribution across the four counties.

Important system limitation:
This prototype estimates measured MLND incidence using the available 45 field records. It does not forecast future outbreaks. The Low, Moderate and High categories are provisional display bands, not officially validated MLND thresholds. The estimate does not replace field inspection, laboratory diagnosis or advice from an agricultural extension officer.
